Delete your data
You request it here, and Dr. Manal carries it out

This is what happens if you send the request:

  • The request goes to Dr. Manal, who carries it out. Until then your account works as usual.
  • When it is carried out, your name, mobile and email are erased and your account is closed, so you can no longer sign in with it.
  • Your certificate numbers remain valid for verification, but without your name.
  • Earlier Ministry of Planning reports show “Removed trainee” in your place.
  • Once it is carried out, nothing can be restored.
