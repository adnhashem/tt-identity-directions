A program designed for your team, or seats for your employees in our cohorts

Two ways for an organization to train its leaders with Dr. Manal Al-Hasawi. Choose what fits the size of your team and your goal. You can also combine them.

A custom in-house program for your team

We start from your organization's question, not a ready-made syllabus. Sessions are built around your sector and your own cases, and delivered at your offices or in our training hall, in Arabic or English.

  1. You send us a requestThe team and its size, the goal, and the period that suits you.
  2. Dr. Manal reviews it herselfShe may call you to understand the goal before proposing anything.
  3. You receive a written quoteWith the proposed program, the number of sessions and the price.
  4. We agree on the datesA private cohort is opened for your team under your organization's name.

Seats for your employees in public cohorts

For a small group, you do not need an in-house program. Your organization's training contact reserves seats in any cohort, names who takes each one, and follows them from the organization portal.
