Did we issue this certificate?

If an employee or a candidate shows you a certificate from Think Tank Strategists, enter its number here to see whether it is genuine and still valid.

Printed at the bottom of the certificate. It starts with the letters TTS. Enter the certificate number first.

If a certificate holder asks us to delete their data, the certificate can still be verified by its number, but without their name.

Sample numbers for this demo: (valid) and (revoked). Any other number is not found.

The result appears here. We show only what a verifier needs: the name, the program, the completion date and the certificate status.
